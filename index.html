Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about tiers, tier tests, points and the Flex City server.

Flex City India's official community PvP platform where players compete in Long Range and Short Range 1v1 matches, submit screenshot proofs, swap ranks, and climb the leaderboard!
After winning a 1v1 match, click the "+ Submit Proof" button, enter your IGN and opponent's name, choose your category, and upload a clear chat screenshot where your opponent agrees to the result.
If you defeat a player ranked higher than you on the leaderboard and have your proof verified by an admin, you take their exact position on the leaderboard!
Total points are the sum of your Long Range points and Short Range points earned through verified match wins.
