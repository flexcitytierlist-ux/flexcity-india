Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Flex City PvP tiers, rankings, and match scores.

Your country is automatically detected when you join or submit a match proof. Your rank applies to both your national leaderboard and the Global leaderboard.
Matches are scored by score differential (Winner Score minus Loser Score) across Overall, Long Range, and Short Range. Lower-ranked winners can also swap ranks and reset the loser's streak upon approval.
You can submit up to 5 match proofs every 8 hours.
Admins enter the player and opponent scores when approving a proof, and it appears on the player's verified match screenshots as well as the top live ticker.
