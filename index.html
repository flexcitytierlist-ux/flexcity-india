What is this?

Welcome to the official community hub tracking every Flex City top player worldwide. Compete in Long Range and Short Range 1v1 PvP duels, climb your country's local ladder, and challenge top contenders on the Global Flex City leaderboard.

📜 How to Submit Proof & Challenge Rules:
  • Chat Screenshot: Upload a clear chat screenshot where your opponent agrees to the 1v1 result & points.
  • Dual Ranking: Winning a match puts you on both your Country Leaderboard and the Global Leaderboard!
  • Direct Challenge: Tap on any player or use the bottom-right Chat icon to message opponents directly for 1v1s.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Flex City PvP tiers, top player rankings, and points.

Your country is automatically detected when you join or submit a match proof. Your points count toward both your national leaderboard and the Global leaderboard simultaneously!
Click on any player's name on the leaderboard and hit "Send Direct Message", or open the Global Chat from the bottom-right message icon to find active opponents!
If you defeat a player ranked higher than you on the leaderboard and have your proof verified by an admin, you take their exact position on the leaderboard!
Total points are the sum of your Long Range points and Short Range points earned through verified match wins.
