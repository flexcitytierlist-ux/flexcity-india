Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Flex City PvP tiers, rankings, and match scores.

Your country is automatically detected when you join or submit a match proof. Your rank applies to both your national leaderboard and the Global leaderboard.
When a lower-ranked player defeats a higher-ranked player and an admin approves the proof, the winner takes the defeated player's rank and the defeated player moves down one rank with their streak reset.
You can submit up to 5 match proofs every 8 hours.
Admins enter the match score when approving a proof, and it appears on the player's verified match screenshots as well as the top live ticker.
